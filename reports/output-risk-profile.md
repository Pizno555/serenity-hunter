## Skill family

Research / citation-heavy skill.

The skill produces investment research output grounded in primary sources.
The main output risks center on evidence integrity, not visual layout.

## Predicted output risks

### Risk 1: Unsupported claims (HIGH)

The skill makes strong judgments ("I would prioritize...", "This ranks higher because...").
Without enforcement, the model may produce confident claims that lack a cited source.

**Failure mode:** A top-ranked candidate is backed by a single social media post or no source at all, but the answer reads as if it is well-evidenced.

**Guard:**
- `references/evidence-ladder.md` now includes an "Unsupported claims guard" section (appended, original content untouched).
- Every top-ranked candidate must cite at least one strong or medium source.
- If no source is available, the claim must be labeled "Needs checking" and the candidate cannot be ranked above "Worth tracking".

### Risk 2: Footnote clutter (MEDIUM)

The workflow targets 25+ sources for deep scans. Without density management, the final answer can become a wall of citations that obscures the reasoning.

**Failure mode:** The answer lists 30 source references inline, making the core judgment hard to find.

**Guard:**
- `references/output-style-and-language.md` now includes a "Citation density management" section (appended, original content untouched).
- Inline citations: max 3 per paragraph.
- Full source list goes in a compact table at the end, not inline.
- Prose leads the judgment; citations support it, not the other way around.

### Risk 3: Evidence-strength inflation (MEDIUM)

The evidence ladder has 4 labels (Strong / Medium / Weak / Needs checking). Under time pressure, the model may upgrade a weak source to medium or a medium to strong to make the answer sound more convincing.

**Failure mode:** A trade publication is labeled "Strong" because it is from a reputable outlet, even though it is not a filing or official disclosure.

**Guard:**
- The scorecard (`scripts/serenity_scorecard.py`) already penalizes evidence quality. The guard reinforces this at the output layer.
- The evidence-ladder guard section includes a strict mapping: only filings, official announcements, transcripts, and regulatory documents qualify as Strong. No exceptions for "reputable media".

### Risk 4: Popular-area downgrade omission (LOW)

The workflow requires naming at least one popular area that ranked lower and explaining why. This is easy to skip when the answer is already long.

**Failure mode:** The answer gives a clean top-5 list without explaining what was excluded and why.

**Guard:**
- `evals/test-cases.md` already checks for this in behavior tests. No additional guard needed.
- The SKILL.md Core Promise chain ends with "what could prove the idea wrong", which implicitly requires considering alternatives.

### Risk 5: Fabricated sources (LOW)

The skill instructs using live sources. If tools are unavailable, the model may invent plausible-sounding filings or announcements.

**Failure mode:** The answer cites "公司 2025 年三季报" that does not exist, or quotes a specific number from an earnings call that never happened.

**Guard:**
- SKILL.md already states: "If unavailable, say which facts need checking and provide the source path."
- The evidence-ladder guard section adds: "Never fabricate a source. If a source cannot be verified, label it 'Needs checking' and provide the search path."
