### Hierarchy quality: GOOD

- Clear top-down flow: conclusion first (Direct view), then supporting evidence, then risk, then action.
- No section is buried below a less important section.
- The "What could weaken the view" section appears before "Next research actions", which is correct — risk before action.

### Table assessment: 2 tables, both adequate

**Table 1 — Evidence (Section 6):**
- Columns: Evidence | Source | Strength | What it supports | What still needs checking
- 5 columns is at the upper bound but acceptable for a research memo.
- "What still needs checking" is a distinctive column that forces intellectual honesty.
- Recommendation (not applied): could add a "Date" column for source recency, but this would push to 6 columns. Current design is fine.

**Table 2 — Repricing events (Section 9):**
- Columns: Event | Expected window | Evidence to monitor | Confidence
- 4 columns, well-balanced.
- "Evidence to monitor" connects the catalyst to a concrete check. Good design.

### Density assessment: APPROPRIATE

- Sections 1-5 are intentionally sparse (labels and single questions). This is correct for a template — the user fills them in.
- Sections 6 and 8 carry the analytical weight and have appropriate density.
- No section is overloaded. No section needs splitting.

### Citation/rhythm assessment: GOOD

- Evidence table is self-contained. No inline citation clutter.
- Financial quality section uses field labels, not prose, keeping it scannable.
- The template does not prescribe citation format, which is correct — citation format belongs in `output-style-and-language.md`.

## Recommendations (for user review, NOT auto-applied)

1. **Valuation snapshot field** (low priority): Add a standalone field in Section 8 for current P/E, P/S, market cap, and 52-week range. Currently implicit.
2. **Next actions trim** (low priority): Consider reducing "Next research actions" from 5 items to 3. Five items rarely get completed; three forces prioritization.
3. **Market-specific variant** (medium priority, future iteration): The template is market-agnostic. A-share answers may need additional fields (问询函, 股权质押, 关联交易). Consider a separate `thesis-template-a-share.md` in a future iteration rather than bloating the base template.
